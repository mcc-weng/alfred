# Beef Chow Mein

fast · ~25 min · serves 2 — planned 2026-06-07

**Ingredients:** 400g beef strips · ¼ green cabbage · 1 carrot, matchsticks · 2 spring onions · 440g fresh egg noodles · sauce: 2 tbsp oyster, 1 tbsp soy, 1 tsp sesame oil

**Steps:** Loosen noodles in hot water. Wok hot: sear beef 2 min, out. Carrot + cabbage 2 min, spring onion. Noodles + sauce + beef back, toss 2 min.
