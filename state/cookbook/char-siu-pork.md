# Char Siu Pork + Greens
🔢 每份 ~40g 蛋白 · ~620 kcal

play · ~1h + marinade · serves 2 + leftovers — planned 2026-06-07 · technique: glaze-basting roast

**Ingredients:** 600g pork scotch fillet, halved into logs · ½ jar char siu sauce + 1 tbsp honey · 2 bunches bok choy · 1 tbsp oyster sauce · rice

**Steps:** Marinate overnight/morning. Oven 200°C, rack on foil tray: 15 min → baste → 10 min → baste → 5–10 min to 68–70°C with charred edges. Rest 10 min, slice thick. Bok choy 90 sec steam, oyster sauce.

💡 Sugar burns — glaze in layers late. Caramelise, don't carbonise.
