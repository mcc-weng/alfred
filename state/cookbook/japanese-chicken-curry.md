# Japanese Chicken Curry

batch ×2 · ~50 min · serves 4 — planned 2026-06-07

**Ingredients:** 700g chicken thigh, chunks · 2 potatoes · 2 carrots · 1 brown onion · ½ box Japanese curry roux (4 blocks) · rice

**Steps:** Brown chicken, out. Onion 2 min, carrot + potato 2 min. Chicken back + 600ml water, simmer 15. Off heat, melt roux. Low 10 min, stirring. Half for dinner, box the rest.
