# Garlic Prawn Spaghetti

fast · ~20 min · serves 2 — planned 2026-06-07

**Ingredients:** 300g raw peeled prawns · 200g spaghetti · 4 garlic cloves, sliced · ½ tsp chilli flakes · parsley · ½ lemon · olive oil

**Steps:** Pasta in salted water, save a mug of pasta water. Low heat: oil + garlic + chilli till just golden. Heat up, prawns 1.5 min/side. Pasta in + pasta water, toss till silky. Off heat: lemon + parsley.
