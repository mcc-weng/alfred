# Crispy-Skin Salmon, Teriyaki Glaze

play · ~35 min · serves 2 — planned 2026-06-07 · technique: crispy skin

**Ingredients:** 2 salmon fillets skin-on ~350g · glaze: 2 tbsp soy, 1 tbsp honey, 1 tbsp sugar, 1 garlic clove, 3 slices ginger, 2 tbsp water · 150g sugar snap peas · rice

**Steps:** Simmer glaze till syrupy ~4 min. Dry skin thoroughly, salt it. Pan medium-high, thin oil, skin down, press 20 sec. Don't touch 5–6 min till cooked ¾ up. Flip 1 min. Plate skin-UP, glaze around not on.

💡 Crispy skin = bone-dry skin + patience; fish releases when ready.
