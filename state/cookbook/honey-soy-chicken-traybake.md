# Honey-Soy Chicken Traybake

fast · ~30 min · serves 2 — planned 2026-06-07

**Ingredients:** 500g chicken thigh fillets halved · 1 bunch broccolini · 2 carrots, coins · sauce: 2 tbsp soy, 1.5 tbsp honey, 2 garlic cloves, 1 tsp sesame oil · rice

**Steps:** Oven 200°C, rice on. Whisk sauce, toss chicken + carrots on lined tray. Roast 20 min. Broccolini in for last 8 min. Spoon juices over, serve on rice.
